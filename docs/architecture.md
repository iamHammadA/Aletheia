- What is knowledge inside Alethia?

- Definition
Knowledge is Aletheia's internal representation of verified scientific understanding. It is composed of entities, relationships, claims, evidence, uncertainty, and reasoning, rather than raw text.

- Purpose
To provide a structured representation of scientific understanding that can be reasoned over, updated, and explained.

- Properties
Dynamic
Evidence-backed
Explainable
Versioned
Continuously updated
Domain independent
