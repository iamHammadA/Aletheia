- What is the problem that Altheia solve?
So basically Alethia is an automonous research scientist. It will parse through all the research papers available and analyse them. Make a plan to extract valuable information from them. Analyse what are the relationships between the parameters important in a study to be solved.

Problems
- It describes what it does, not the problem.
- "Parse all research papers" is unrealistic.
- "Extract valuable information" is too vague.
- It doesn't explain why current research is difficult.

Think about the real pain.

Researchers face:

- Millions of papers.
- Contradictory findings.
- Hidden relationships.
- Missed research opportunities.
- Too much information for any human to absorb.

Aletheia exists because human researchers cannot keep up with the growth of scientific knowledge.

- Why dies the world need it?
Scientific knowledge is expanding at a rate that exceeds any individual's ability to comprehend it. Researchers must navigate millions of publications, fragmented findings, conflicting evidence, and rapidly evolving fields, making it increasingly difficult to identify meaningful relationships, evaluate the strength of evidence, and discover new research opportunities. This information overload slows scientific progress and leaves valuable insights undiscovered.

- What makes Aletheia different?
Unlike conventional AI assistants that summarize or retrieve information, Aletheia constructs a persistent, evolving representation of scientific knowledge. It continuously integrates evidence from multiple publications, identifies relationships and contradictions, evaluates the strength of scientific claims, generates evidence-backed hypotheses, and supports reproducible scientific reasoning through transparent multi-agent collaboration.
